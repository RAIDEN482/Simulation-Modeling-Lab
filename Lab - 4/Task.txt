Perform a two-sample t-test on the following data:

Data 1: [18.4, 19.1, 17.9, 18.7, 18.3]
Data 2: [20.2, 20.5, 20.1, 20.3, 20.4]
Use significance level α = 0.05 to determine whether the population means differ.

Tasks
State  H0  and  H1 . You will assume equal variances (pooled).
Compute the sample means, sample variances (ddof = 1), and the mean difference.
Calculate the t-statistic, degrees of freedom, and the two-tailed p-value.
Construct the 95% confidence interval for the mean difference.
Decision & Interpretation: Conclude (reject / fail to reject  H0 ) and explain the result in one or two plain-English sentences.
